Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who uses AI House

AI House is a single-user application that runs on the owner's own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

2. What data is handled

  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok, X, or other platforms for the owner's own accounts, stored only in a local database on the owner's computer.
  • The basic profile information of the owner's own connected account (such as the display name), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Videos, images, and captions created by the owner, used only to publish them to the owner's own accounts.

3. What is not done

AI House does not collect data about other TikTok or X users, does not sell or share data with third parties, does not use data for advertising, and does not send tokens anywhere other than the platform that issued them.

4. Retention and removal

Tokens and content stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke AI House's access at any time in the security settings of TikTok or X, and can delete the account connection inside the application.
