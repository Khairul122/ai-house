AI House

AI House is a personal content dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps one person plan, create, review, and publish content about AI technology to their own social media accounts.

What it does

  • Drafts captions, images, and videos with AI assistants.
  • Shows every draft to the owner for review.
  • Publishes to the owner's own accounts (for example TikTok and X) only after the owner presses the publish button.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service and has no other users.
  • It never posts automatically and never accesses accounts that do not belong to the owner.
  • It does not collect, sell, or share data about other people.
